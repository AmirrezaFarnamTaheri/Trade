## Local contract checks

The local fixtures exercise receipt-ordered execution, partial depth, maker activation and cancellation, future-input perturbation, tied factor ranks, tail-count refusal and deterministic covariance cases. They establish TradingGYM behavior only. The remaining upstream comparisons, license review for proposed adoption, and measured net decision improvement remain open. These comparisons do not require another registered research slot.

## Fixed-input differential results

**R011, trend forecast.** Ran the actual pysystemtrade `ewmac` function at commit `bbf5cf823e16be1cca27493a94099eb8cf33f6e7` with pandas 3.1.1. For 512 daily prices given by `100 + sin(i/8) + 0.01*i`, starting at zero, both implementations received the same three spans, sample standard deviation of the preceding 36 price changes, equal weights, scalar 10 and ±20 cap. Across the 256 post-warmup forecasts the largest absolute difference was 3.1455322593. Both final forecasts were 20. The upstream pandas EWMA uses adjusted weights and TradingGYM uses a recursive first-price initialization. Keep the historical TradingGYM convention and do not interchange these forecasts. Hummingbot maker lifecycle remains open.

**R013, expected shortfall.** Used fixed 60%/40% weights and two deterministic asset returns at index i, `((37*i)%103-51)/1000` and `((19*i)%71-35)/1000`, starting at zero. At 95% confidence on 200 observations, TradingGYM and skfolio 1.14.4 both returned a positive loss of 0.03684. On 211 observations, TradingGYM returned 0.0377 and skfolio returned 0.03742369668246445. TradingGYM takes ten whole observations and skfolio assigns fractional mass at the boundary. Use the same empirical tail convention before comparing optimizers. These samples measure numerical agreement only.

**R010, Wallex maker queue.** Used the BTCUSDT bid at 2026-11-21 21:51:36.071676 UTC, 60382.74 for 0.00250182 BTC. A sell-aggressor print of 0.00033643 BTC at the bid had event time 21:51:37 and receipt time 21:51:39.349921 UTC. Ran hftbacktest v1.8.4 RiskAverseQueueModel (20cd9470a431e90c526eca6975ef389073c9aca5) and the TradingGYM maker queue on that one price level, with one BTC requested. Both left 0.00216539 BTC ahead and reported no fill. This covers queue depletion at one price. LEAN fee accounting, Nautilus event matching, cancel races and multi-level fills remain open.

**R012, tied factor scores.** Evaluated the unmodified Qlib calc_ic function in isolation at revision 54355232463878d2eebb91fe0ee5fa7fa1f5976c. Three dates each had four assets with scores [1,1,2,2] and labels [1,2,3,4]. Qlib returned 0.8944271909999159 for both Pearson IC and Rank-IC. TradingGYM returned 0.894427 for both after its documented output rounding. The comparison excludes the Qlib loader and training pipeline.

**R013, risk and allocations.** On the 200/211-observation samples above, Riskfolio-Lib CVaR_Hist at 344b899eb72e5636330647f08e3c5d88a8f71a84 (unmodified function executed in isolation) returned 0.03684/0.03742369668246445. PyPortfolioOpt 1.6.0 EfficientCVaR, with both asset weights fixed, returned 0.0368399999802/0.0374236960827.

For a held-out allocation comparison, added a third daily return, ((31*i)%83-41)/1000. Fit single-linkage hierarchical risk parity on rows 0–199 using sample covariance. Held the resulting weights on rows 200–249 and charged 5 bp on entry and liquidation. TradingGYM and PyPortfolioOpt allocated 22.859948%, 44.522156% and 32.617895%. From one quote unit both returned 0.9193309732 net, with 1.9192911185 turnover. No measured improvement justifies a production dependency on this fixture. The public PyPortfolioOpt HRP entry point failed with SciPy 1.18.1 because it reads a removed private linkage registry. The comparison used SciPy single linkage with PyPortfolioOpt's own leaf-ordering and allocation methods. Additional market regimes and real execution costs remain untested.
