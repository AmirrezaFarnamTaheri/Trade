How this research workspace works

A source-grounded guide to observation, paper research and protected boundaries.

ACT 1 — WHAT

Watch before acting

TradingGYM is a notebook for market observations and paper experiments.

It observes Wallex spot markets. No established trading edge is claimed.
ACT 2 — WHO

Meet the six desks

The reading desk, laboratory, archive, adviser, market and locked vault stay separate.

The browser presents evidence. Python researches. Node advises. Rust protects custody.
ACT 3 — OBSERVE

Keep the original evidence

The collector records quotes and when they become available.

Unavailable history stays unavailable. Synthetic demonstration data remains labelled and isolated.
ACT 4 — REPLAY

Try an idea on paper

Research replays only the observations available at each decision.

Fees, spread, latency and gaps shape modeled results. Paper fills are not venue fills.
ACT 5 — REVIEW

Read the result and limits

Saved reports expose exact values, costs, evidence and uncertainty.

A good paper result never grants capital permission. Independent evaluation remains a separate gate.
ACT 6 — COLLABORATE

Pass notes through narrow doors

Python receives browser requests and stores reports. Node returns advice through a private channel.

Protected helpers use OS-authenticated pipes. Advisory output cannot become an order.
ACT 7 — SUPERPOWERS

Exact, causal, inspectable

Decimal money, retained history and explicit boundaries keep evidence understandable.

Immutable ledger bytes reveal tampering. Missing evidence remains a visible limitation.

Security Features

Loopback and tokens

Public reads remain local. Private reads and writes require the session token.

Separate principals

Windows service custody and helper access use actual OS identities and protected ACLs.

Exact money

Python uses Decimal; native execution uses integer quanta. Stored ledger bytes remain unchanged.

Explicit uncertainty

Missing acknowledgements can follow a commit. Inspect before any manual retry.

No authority promotion

Paper returns, model advice and account receipts cannot authorize funded orders.

Code Quality

Tools configured: pytest · Node test · Cargo test · Ruff · Clippy · rustfmt. Configuration is not a passing result.

Research capabilities

These features preserve evidence without granting trading authority.

Causal replay

Decision-time knowledge and receipt clocks prevent future data from appearing early.

Exact costs and balances

Inspect decimal values and modeled fees, spread and latency separately.

Bounded advisory research

Fixed actions, process deadlines and explicit paid prompts preserve the research boundary.

Traceable records

Saved runs, trial ledgers, observation vintages and evidence digests retain provenance.

Current scope

Public observations and paper research are available. Funded execution and Autopilot remain unavailable.
