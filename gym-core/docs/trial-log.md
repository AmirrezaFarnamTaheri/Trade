# Trial log

One question governs every entry: is there a net edge on Wallex after costs, against holding USDT?

Each hypothesis is written here with its data and pass mark **before** it is run. The result is added afterwards and
the pass mark is never edited. A new idea is a new entry, so the count of things tried stays honest.

Costs used throughout, per side: the account rate read from `/v1/account/fee` (0.10%), and the published level-1
rates from `/v2/structured-fee` (Toman markets 0.25% maker, 0.30% taker; USDT markets 0.10% maker, 0.13% taker).

## Method notes

- `thin-market-quoting` to `btc-daily-trend` are screens on tables, run by `scripts/edge_screen.py`; nothing in them is fitted. `logistic-entry-gate` is the first registered model fit under the 2026-11-14 PRODUCT.md rule. `ten-coin-trend` is a table again.
- `thin-market-quoting` looks at every watched market, so its bar is t at least 4, not 2.
- `btc-daily-trend` trades at the daily close with an assumed 0.10% for half the spread. The rule family (trend on the two largest coins) was chosen in 2026 by someone who knows those coins rose; the trial count does not correct for that.
- `ten-coin-trend` and `btc-daily-trend` agree on the weak point: outside 2024 the trend rule lost money in both later years, on one coin and on ten. The `btc-daily-trend` pass is one year's result.
- The screens use floating point. Anything that passes is replayed in the exact paper engine before it counts.
- Power (2026-11-18, `evidence/2026-11-18-detectable-edge.md`): no daily test so far could detect an edge below 10 bps a day
  (about 44% a year), far above a realistic 1 to 5 bps. Their FAILs mean "not proven". New registrations state their smallest
  detectable edge, computed with `scripts/detectable_edge.py`, before they run.
- `passive-multi-asset-mix` used futures as stand-ins for silver, oil and copper; the Wallex tokens track the funds SLV, USO
  and COPX. Rerun on those funds as a sensitivity check (not a new score): +306.6%, Sharpe 0.98, probability 0.999
  (`evidence/2026-11-18-tokenised-premiums.md`).
- Programme score: the overview corrects the best result for every completed trial (Benjamini-Hochberg). A trial with no
  stated probability enters as p = 1.
- Sweeps (`gymctl paper-sweep`) are exploration, not trials. The 2026-11-17 sweep ran 20,345 configurations: 16 strategies, 4 bar sizes, every market, 31 Oct to 16 Nov 2026. It has no pass mark, and its best rows are what luck produces at that count. An idea taken from a sweep becomes a trial only when it is registered here, with data the sweep did not use; `trend-with-short-side` was registered that way.

## Why they lost

| Idea | Cause | What would have to change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intraday reversion (`usdttmn-intraday-reversion`) | No effect to harvest: the gross edge was about zero before any fee. | A different signal, not a cheaper fee. |
| Quoting thin markets (`thin-market-quoting`) | Wide spreads are wide because the resting side is picked off; where the sums were positive the touch held 15 to 32 USDT. | Size, which those markets do not have. |
| Price gaps (`triangle-gap`, `zero-fee-round-trip`, `otc-against-book`) | The gap is smaller than the cost of crossing it: three taker fees (`triangle-gap`), a 0.97% spread (`zero-fee-round-trip`), a 0.75% to 1.4% markup (`otc-against-book`). | A fee tier or product the account does not have. |
| Strongest coin or cash (`strongest-coin-or-cash`) | Ranking was carried by large coins in the 2020-21 rally; on smaller, younger coins the strongest one often reversed, and holding one volatile coin turns a slightly positive average day into a large compounded loss. | Size by volatility or hold a few strong coins; test on coins the ranking period can stand in for. |
| Book imbalance (`book-imbalance`) | The imbalance predicts a few bps of movement; taking liquidity costs the whole spread plus two fees, 10 to 50 times more. | Use the signal to rest on the right side rather than cross, on a market with a tight spread (USDTTMN), at a fee the account can get. |
| Choosing among rules (`rule-selector-with-cash`) | Monthly re-selection follows whichever rule just won, so it arrives after the move; the best single rule, known only in hindsight, did better in 27 of 30 cells. | A selector that scores rules on something that persists, not last month's return. |
| Long breakouts and funding as confirmation (`spot-breakout-long`, `funding-confirmed-trend`) | Altcoins fell 85% on average in 2024 to 2026; both rules lost far less than holding, and funding confirmation turned the trend rule positive, but neither result is strong enough to rule out luck over 1,035 days. | A forward record: run `funding-confirmed-trend` on paper from now and judge it on days nobody has seen. |
| Funding as a crowding warning (`funding-crowding-filter`) | High funding came with the strongest uptrends, so standing aside then removed the trend rule's best days. | Read funding as confirmation, not warning, and test that on the held-out coins as a new registration. |
| Short breakouts on margin (`margin-breakout-long-short`) | Breakout longs paid; breakout shorts lost even when held for days, and margin fees and liquidations made 2x worse than 1x. | Long-only breakouts on spot at 1x, as a new registration on the unscored held-out coins. |
| Shorting below the trend (`trend-with-short-side`) | Coins spent more time rallying than falling in 2018 to 2023, and a short pays about 33% a year to borrow plus a fresh entry every 21 days; 2x shorts were liquidated in rallies. The 17-day fall that suggested it was one window. | A short held only for days, not months, with a signal that predicts the fall itself. |
| Daily trend (`venue-daily-trend`, `btc-daily-trend`, `ten-coin-trend`) | The trend rule earns in a strong rising year and gives part back otherwise; daily rebalancing of ten coins cost 14% over the scored period. | Evidence from a period nobody has seen, which is the forward record. |
| Fitted model (`logistic-entry-gate`) | Price and volume history alone did not predict the next five days. | Inputs the failed trials did not use: order-book imbalance, the implied Toman premium. |

Round-trip cost on this account is 0.20% in fees plus the spread. Every short-horizon idea so far had a gross edge
below that, so the remaining candidates are slow rules (few trades) or signals with new information.

## Forward record
