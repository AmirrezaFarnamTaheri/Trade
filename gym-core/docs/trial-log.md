# Trial log

One question governs every entry: is there a net edge on Wallex after costs, against holding USDT?

Each hypothesis is written here with its data and pass mark **before** it is run. The result is added afterwards and
the pass mark is never edited. A new idea is a new entry, so the count of things tried stays honest.

Costs used throughout, per side: the account rate read from `/v1/account/fee` (0.10%), and the published level-1
rates from `/v2/structured-fee` (Toman markets 0.25% maker, 0.30% taker; USDT markets 0.10% maker, 0.13% taker).

## Method notes

- T2 to T6 are screens on tables, run by `scripts/edge_screen.py`; nothing in them is fitted. T8 is the first registered model fit under the 2026-11-14 PRODUCT.md rule. T9 is a table again.
- T2 looks at every watched market, so its bar is t at least 4, not 2.
- T6 trades at the daily close with an assumed 0.10% for half the spread. The rule family (trend on the two largest coins) was chosen in 2026 by someone who knows those coins rose; the trial count does not correct for that.
- T9 and T6 agree on the weak point: outside 2024 the trend rule lost money in both later years, on one coin and on ten. The T6 pass is one year's result.
- The screens use floating point. Anything that passes is replayed in the exact paper engine before it counts.
- Sweeps (`gymctl paper-sweep`) are exploration, not trials. The 2026-11-17 sweep ran 20,345 configurations: 16 strategies, 4 bar sizes, every market, 31 Oct to 16 Nov 2026. It has no pass mark, and its best rows are what luck produces at that count. An idea taken from a sweep becomes a trial only when it is registered here, with data the sweep did not use; T11 was registered that way.

## Why they lost

| Idea | Cause | What would have to change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intraday reversion (T1) | No effect to harvest: the gross edge was about zero before any fee. | A different signal, not a cheaper fee. |
| Quoting thin markets (T2) | Wide spreads are wide because the resting side is picked off; where the sums were positive the touch held 15 to 32 USDT. | Size, which those markets do not have. |
| Price gaps (T3, T5, T7) | The gap is smaller than the cost of crossing it: three taker fees (T3), a 0.97% spread (T5), a 0.75% to 1.4% markup (T7). | A fee tier or product the account does not have. |
| Choosing among rules (T12) | Monthly re-selection follows whichever rule just won, so it arrives after the move; the best single rule, known only in hindsight, did better in 27 of 30 cells. | A selector that scores rules on something that persists, not last month's return. |
| Shorting below the trend (T11) | Coins spent more time rallying than falling in 2018 to 2023, and a short pays about 33% a year to borrow plus a fresh entry every 21 days; 2x shorts were liquidated in rallies. The 17-day fall that suggested it was one window. | A short held only for days, not months, with a signal that predicts the fall itself. |
| Daily trend (T4, T6, T9) | The trend rule earns in a strong rising year and gives part back otherwise; daily rebalancing of ten coins cost 14% over the scored period. | Evidence from a period nobody has seen, which is the forward record. |
| Fitted model (T8) | Price and volume history alone did not predict the next five days. | Inputs the failed trials did not use: order-book imbalance, the implied Toman premium. |

Round-trip cost on this account is 0.20% in fees plus the spread. Every short-horizon idea so far had a gross edge
below that, so the remaining candidates are slow rules (few trades) or signals with new information.

## Forward record

T6 earned a prospective paper run, which started on 2026-11-14 for BTCUSDT with 1,000 USDT of paper cash:
`gymctl allocate BTCUSDT --vol-target 5 --band 0`, once a day, installed by `scripts/windows/Install-AllocationJournal.ps1`.
Decisions append to `<data>/live/allocation/journal.jsonl` and cannot be re-decided. The first decision was to stay
in USDT (close 68,175 against a 50-day average of 80,495). This record, not the backtest, is the evidence that counts.
