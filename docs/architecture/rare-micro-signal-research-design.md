# Rare micro-signals across markets

Research proposal for the paper engine. Seek additional independently timed, executable opportunities per week at a fixed portfolio risk budget. Use the existing rare momentum ignition, pullback rejection and coiled-down short as incumbents. Scan each point-in-time eligible venue/instrument with the detectors its data supports. A signal can earn a place through time diversification even when it trades only one family.

## Candidate detectors

These thresholds, horizons and directions are proposed first specifications. Freeze them on development data. Quantiles use only prior eligible observations and are conditioned on venue, liquidity and time of week where sample size permits. Pool across comparable instruments when a single name lacks history. Persist the reference sample and mark insufficient history explicitly.

| Detector and mechanism | Observable sequence and paper action | Expiry, failure and control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Failed downside auction. A brief liquidation exhausts sellers. | On closed 5-minute bars, price breaches the low of the prior 24 bars in a bottom-1% volatility-scaled down move. Within two bars it closes back inside the range. Buy at the first subsequent executable ask with an ordinary spread. | Cancel on a new low before entry. Exit on another close below the old low or after six bars. Compare with equally deep selloffs without a reclaim. |
| Peer-relative catch-up. A liquid related instrument reprices late. | Fit each instrument's sensitivity to a liquid family basket using prior days, excluding the instrument, its wrappers and same-underlying pairs. On a 15-minute top-1% positive basket shock, require broad participation and a bottom-5% target residual. Buy only after the target's fresh bid starts rising while the basket holds. | Cancel if the basket gives back half its shock. Exit at half residual closure or after four bars. Compare with ordinary residual dips; use a beta-matched paper control to isolate the catch-up payoff. |
| Idiosyncratic strength during a common selloff. A buyer persists through stress. | When family breadth falls into its historical bottom 5%, identify instruments with ordinary spreads, recent trades and a positive residual. Require the target to hold its pre-shock low through a second down bar in the basket. Buy on the first subsequent quote after breadth stabilizes. | Exit if the target loses its defended low or after one hour. Compare with equally liquid low-beta names on the same shock. Limit the whole selloff to one risk allocation. |
| Sell-flow absorption. Sellers keep hitting a defended bid. | Require a burst of signed sell prints in the top 1% relative to available depth, repeated bid replenishment, and unusually small downward price impact. Buy after the bid steps up and persists across two fresh updates. | Entry expires within 30 seconds; exit at a broken defended bid or after two minutes. Match by sell intensity and spread without replenishment. Requires ordered trades and book updates; coarse polling cannot identify it. |
| Post-shock liquidity recovery. Trading becomes affordable before a dislocation fully closes. | After a local spread reaches its top 1%, require recovery below the prior 75th percentile and replenishment on both sides. If a synchronized independent reference is still higher by more than all-in costs plus a frozen buffer, buy at the local ask. | Cancel on reference staleness, depth withdrawal or convergence before entry. Exit on executable convergence or a two-minute timeout. Compare with the same basis when spreads have been normal throughout. |
| Equivalent-exposure substitution. A held asset can be exchanged for more units of comparable exposure. | For a pre-existing gold sleeve, sell the held token at its native bid and buy the substitute at its native ask only when both sizes cover the intended trade and net backing-adjusted units gained exceed the frozen hurdle. | Expire when either leg ages out. Price the residual if one leg misses; account for issuer divergence and an eventual switch back. Compare final executable wealth with holding the original token. This extends the existing gold study. |

Two-way variants require separate evidence by direction. A bearish spot signal may reduce held inventory. A true short needs a point-in-time borrow offer, available size and carry in the paper account. Detectors must abstain when the feed or instrument lacks a required field.

## Scheduling and evidence

1. Each detector returns a timestamped intent with instrument, venue, mechanism, event ID, direction, first eligible execution, expiry, invalidation, size and required inputs. Repeated ticks update an existing event. Rearm only after the defining condition clears. Compute cheap filters on a new closed bar or relevant quote update; run expensive detectors only for armed candidates.
2. Combine signals by union. Count same-time, same-direction BTC signals as one opportunity. Group correlated instruments by common shock, keep separate venue inventory, and bound total risk per episode. Opposing signals first reduce existing exposure under an explicit netting rule. Agreement can be tested as a separate feature after enough examples accrue.
3. Before fitting returns, shadow-log all intents, expiries and missing data. For each family publish eligible instrument-hours, trigger count, unique episodes, days active, overlap with the incumbent, fills at intended size and weeks with zero fills. Add families in a frozen order and also remove each one from the full portfolio to expose order dependence.
4. Replay every arm from the same capital and frozen tape, with execution after observation, available depth, fees, partial fills, portfolio limits and cash settlement. Report net P&L per intended opportunity including misses, incremental weekly net wealth, capital-hours, expected shortfall, and the largest episode's contribution. Preserve tradeable side and size during valuation. Stress a delayed update and double the observed spread.
5. Select on development data and purge overlapping holding periods at validation boundaries. Reserve chronological blocks and entire instrument families for transfer tests. Infer uncertainty by resampling common time blocks across symbols together. Track every detector and threshold trial. Freeze one candidate portfolio for a genuinely prospective comparison; insufficient independent episodes yields an inconclusive verdict. Set a practical frequency, risk and net-wealth improvement target before that window begins.

First implementation slice: add the failed auction and peer catch-up alongside existing ignition on the same bar stream. Produce an overlap and opportunity report before adding model selection. Shadow-collect native gold quote pairs in parallel. Defer the absorption detector until ordered prints and book replenishment can be observed. The empirical motivation for considering flow relative to depth comes from equities and requires independent validation on these venues. [Cont, Kukanov and Stoikov](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.6402)
## Design decisions the six detectors leave open

| Design choice | Paper experiment | What would change the design |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Select signals for the weeks they add to the portfolio. Two individually good detectors may always compete for the same collateral. | On development blocks, select a small set by incremental net wealth and reduction in long inactive stretches at fixed risk. Compare with selecting the same number by standalone performance. Freeze both sets and evaluate on later blocks, including flat weeks. | Keep the coverage objective only if it adds independently timed, economically worthwhile fills. More trades alone earns no credit. |
| Separate a sound setup from an exhausted trade. A thesis can fail before a stop is hit, or survive ordinary price noise. | For the incumbent ignition entries, write down what should happen next if buying pressure is real. Freeze one failure condition, such as no bid advance after a specified amount of market activity despite continuing buy flow. Fork each open paper lot into incumbent exit and thesis-failure exit. Follow both to the original deadline, then replay the portfolio with released cash. | Adopt an exit specialist if avoided losses and redeployed capital exceed extra spread and prematurely closed winners on held-out episodes. This can improve the existing book with zero new entries. |
| Treat confirmation as something the trader pays for. Waiting reveals information while the executable price can move away. | At each first alert shadow three equal-budget actions: enter immediately, wait for confirmation, and take half now with the remainder conditional. Keep the same expiry and record all alerts, including those never confirmed. | Compare net wealth per original alert. If confirmed trades look better but the waiting policy earns less, retain the earlier action. |
| Calibrate rarity at the scanner level. A rare threshold on one pair can produce a routine extreme somewhere in a large universe. | Replay nested point-in-time universes. Use synchronized time blocks that preserve cross-asset shocks to estimate the frequency of extreme alerts under a prespecified null. Duplicate one instrument under a synthetic alias as an invariant test. | Duplicating a listing must add zero independent risk. Adding genuinely different markets should increase executable coverage after correcting for searching a larger universe. |
| Separate whether a signal transfers from where the resulting exposure should be held. The best sensor and the best trading venue can differ. | From the same timestamped signal compare executing the source instrument, a prefunded liquid equivalent, and abstention. Enforce source latency, quote freshness, basis risk, fees, inventory ownership and available size. | If the liquid equivalent retains the move after costs, use the source as a sensor. If the advantage exists only in an inaccessible quote, count a detection and zero executable opportunities. |
| Estimate how many real events the recorder misses. A silent scanner can mean a quiet market or insufficient observations. | During overlapping high-resolution coverage, run the existing recorder cadence and a full-resolution shadow detector. For every missed event, measure duration, executable lifetime, spread and attainable size. Include outages separately. | Spend on faster collection only where recoverable missed opportunities survive costs. Publish an unknown-coverage bucket for intervals with no high-resolution reference. |

Start the exit and confirmation experiments on one frozen stream of incumbent entries. They reuse known opportunities and reveal whether the current bottleneck is discovering trades, arriving in time, or holding them too long.
