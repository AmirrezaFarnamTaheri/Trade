# Promoting research strategies into the paper engine

Design recommendation against `a5e4086`. The first release should reproduce the slow S1+S5 comparator through a versioned paper entry point. Run ignition independently in shadow. Give one challenger permission to request unused capital under a frozen reservation rule. Defer any change to the default paper composition until paired evidence supports it. Funded execution remains outside this design.

## Use the existing engine at the right boundary

The [paper strategy registry](../../gym-core/src/gym/domain/registry.py) already validates parameters and supports regime and exit wrappers. Its [signal blend](../../gym-core/src/gym/domain/blend_strategies.py) is suitable for members observing the same instrument and decision clock. The [daily allocation service](../../gym-core/src/gym/services/allocation.py) initializes holdings per symbol. A 1,000-USDT shared portfolio cannot be created by invoking that service independently on eighteen symbols. A multi-instrument strategy needs an explicit account-level entry point.

1. Extract the exact slow policy behind its research runner into a callable used by research and incremental paper replay. Retain its cross-sectional inputs and rebalance calendar. Register an explicit portfolio configuration referencing versioned sleeves, account, restrictions and schedule. Reuse the existing single-symbol registry for compatible components. Verify the public paper entry point, not only direct function calls.
2. Use an immutable input cut for each event. The slow policy emits desired portfolio exposures on its own clock. Fast detectors emit expiring, instrument-specific intents. Record origin, event/episode, direction, sizing basis, valid-until, invalidation, permissible venues and exit owner. Distinguish “no new opinion”, “data inadequate”, “maintain exposure” and “exit”. Missing a fast update must never liquidate a slow position by accident.
3. Resolve opposing or concurrent requests in a deterministic allocator with one account balance per venue and asset. Compute all sales and purchases from one snapshot, enforce settlement, share displayed depth and attribute internal offsets to both virtual sleeves. A fast exit releases only its own units. Protective reductions retain priority when new entries are suspended.
4. Bind code, model and thresholds, universe, data watermark, fees, venue rules and evidence IDs to one composition version through the [experiment registry](../../gym-core/src/gym/services/experiment_registry.py). Separate opt-in availability, prospective challenger status and default paper selection. A rollback creates a new event, stops new entries for the retired version and hands its existing lots to their registered exit policy.

## The first conditional hybrid

Test two independent sleeves competing for a common account. Hold signals, expiry, entry logic and exits fixed. The sole experimental change is fast-entry capital admission. Compute available cash as settled venue cash less existing commitments, fees and a slow reserve fixed from development data. A fast order may use the excess only if the venue can fill the minimum size, the signal is still fresh, and the common-shock exposure cap permits it. Permit a partial slice under an explicit minimum; log every rejected or reduced request.

Compare slow alone, fast alone, both with ordinary sharing, and both with the reservation rule. Include one fixed split, such as the already studied 70/30, as a control with the same aggregate risk cap. Do not optimize the split on the final window. Evaluate net terminal wealth and paired weekly differences alongside drawdown, tail loss, unpriced inventory, missed slow trades, opportunity drought and turnover. A reserve can starve a profitable fast trade while protecting cash that the slow book never uses; attribute both costs.

Distinguish three condition types. **Feasibility** such as cash, depth or available borrow is enforced for every run. **Capital admission** is an allocator decision and is measured by the experiment above. **Predictive gating** such as a breadth state or ML probability is a separate research hypothesis. Compare the ungated, gated and exposure-matched mechanically thinned portfolios. A gate that improves drawdown merely by holding cash must beat the cheap cash alternative on the chosen objective. For predictive regimes use as-of inputs, separate enter/leave thresholds and predeclared handling of uncertain transitions. Include regime changes during an open trade.

## Promotion and rollback evidence

- **Implementation parity.** First fix the known per-asset inventory, forward rebalance ordering and missing-mark errors. Test symbol renaming, a missing bar during an open lot, two sleeves requesting the same depth, delayed settlement, fees on the final event, duplicate delivery and process restart. Require matching event-level holdings, cash and reason codes between batch and incremental paths.
- **Economic increment.** Before evaluation, specify the incumbent, smallest worthwhile net gain or insurance benefit, tolerable drawdown/tail deterioration, cost and delay assumptions, and independent active-block requirement. Derive the margin from the owner's objective and measured costs. Count all gates, blends and rejected alternatives in the trial register. Repeated selection increases the chance of backtest overfitting. [Bailey et al.](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf)
- **Prospective paper challenge.** Freeze one candidate and the incumbent, start both with identical capital, record every decision before its outcome and use the [holdout ledger](../../gym-core/src/gym/services/holdout_ledger.py). Review paired blocks at a preregistered date or using a valid sequential rule. If the interval cannot distinguish the agreed margin, continue only within the evidence budget; otherwise archive as inconclusive. A single catastrophic reconciliation failure suspends new entries independently of statistical performance.
- **Release.** A named paper configuration becomes the default only after parity, economic and prospective criteria pass and its receipt identifies the exact composition. Keep the incumbent running in parallel for an agreed comparison period. Monitor intended versus filled exposure, slippage, outages, concentration, missing marks and settlement. Document whether each trigger pauses entries, restores the incumbent or requires controlled liquidation. Preserve pending orders and lot ownership across rollback.

## Three extensions worth testing later

- **Promote a substitution rather than a new bet.** Let a gold specialist improve the instrument used to express an already approved exposure. Measure additional executable gold units after all costs against holding the original token. The specialist receives no independent directional allocation.
- **Run a shadow auction for occupied slots.** When two candidates want the same collateral, fork the ledger at that instant. Award the slot to each candidate in turn, then run both branches through the same subsequent market tape. Learn where a simple priority rule actually matters before training a router. Use the future outcomes for evaluation only.
- **Promote a detector into a narrower role.** A poor standalone entry may be a useful reason to delay an existing purchase, reduce its size, or exit early. Freeze one role and compare it with an equally active simple control. Record it as a new trial. An unsuccessful entry experiment alone provides no evidence for the new role.
