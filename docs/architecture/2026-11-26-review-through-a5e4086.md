# Review of the four updates through a5e4086

**Decision.** The three-signal union delivered substantially more activity, but the published paper result gives no reason to adopt it. Repair accounting and controls before ranking the mechanisms. Reviewed local commits `00fb859`, `3e2b6a8`, `256da2f` and `a5e4086` against `113528c`. The range changes 28 files. Targeted tests and two independent accounting probes were run locally. The research receipts are the committed runs.

In a two-book probe with different instruments priced at 100 and 1,000, a 125 USDT initial balance became **1,247.80** after one buy and one sell, without either asset appreciating. ## What improved and what the results support

- The journal now marks previously held units before applying the new target, and the append helper checks the slot while holding its lock. The original daily weighting error and duplicate physical append have concrete repairs. Concurrent callers still ignore the helper's false return and can report a duplicate as recorded.
- The candle reader applies the as-of bound before limiting. Gold bar tests now carry an explicit cutoff, compare execution timings and reprice the issuer shock. Prefunding now debits spending cash and delays settlement. These changes are worth preserving when fixing asset identity.
- The cadence study now measures process CPU and p95 loop time and identifies missed triggers. G3 can run its safety suite via the CLI. Externally supplied G3 JSON still earns a displayed pass from its status string without verifying freshness or code identity. The watchdog checks future bar labels on the latest row. A missing registration/hash can still leave model status OK. Treat those states separately from verified identity.
- **Rare signals:** the union filled in 128 of 156 weeks versus 54 for ignition and reduced the longest drought from 18 to 9 weeks. Reported returns were −43.59% versus −3.80%. Increased activity was real within the simulation. The tested two-hour approximations differ materially from the proposed five-minute failed auction and fifteen-minute peer-relative setup. Keep the economic verdict scoped to these implementations. [Weekly receipt](../../evidence/studies/v3-adaptive-engine/rare-micro-signal-portfolio-study.md)
- **Allocation:** both sleeves together ended at 1,142.81 versus 1,158.28 for slow alone, a marginal difference of −15.47 USDT. The reported −67.05 USDT is the four-arm interaction and includes capital competition, sizing and subsequent path differences. It cannot all be attributed to the 58 blocked fast orders. **Confirmation:** on the fixed-horizon alert diagnostic, immediate entry beat waiting by 44.60 bps per alert. Follow that on the same position-sized account after fixing forward allocation. [Diagnostic receipt](../../evidence/studies/v3-adaptive-engine/cross-layer-design-diagnostics.md)
- **Stress:** the BTC equivalent and low-beta control use the identical BTC trade by construction. Shared dependency outputs are literal constants. The TMN withdrawal is arithmetic on an initial USDT balance and three FX marks. These are illustrative scenarios; they do not establish that a strategy can meet a withdrawal during a venue outage. [Stress implementation](../../gym-core/src/gym/services/experiments/remaining_cross_layer_suite_study.py#L124)

**Validation.** Across two invocations, 46 targeted tests passed, covering the audit counterexamples, forward journal, smart execution and health, safety boundaries and v2 champion. The two independent probes above ran against current implementation functions. The full regression suite and all study pipelines were not rerun for this review.
