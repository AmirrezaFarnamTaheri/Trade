# Whole-market research plan

**Decision this plan must support.** Given one account, a stated capital amount and the information available by each decision deadline, determine whether changing the portfolio improved realizable wealth after costs relative to feasible alternatives. A completed replay can support a negative or inconclusive research result. Qualification of the software, evidence for a particular strategy, and permission to send orders are separate decisions.

**Current starting point.** The November 14–21 development sweep observed 373 markets, qualified 342, traded 337 and had 318 quote-currency losses under the displayed-size model. Each market had independent capital. A two-market shared USDT replay ended at 964.39 from 1,000. These answer different questions and supply regression examples for different runners. Existing single-symbol jobs retain their recorded settings. The whole-market default below applies to the proposed project runner. [Earlier audit](../evidence/2026-11-22-historical-replay-financial-audit.md)

**Critical path.** Deliver the universe manifest and a hand-checkable cash ledger first, a deterministic spot replay second, and a full-week result with measured resource use third. Release additional product families independently when their lifecycle fixtures pass. Start comparative strategy research after the relevant replay path passes. The exploratory backlog is optional.

## Capability contract

The core identifies instruments by venue, product, base, quote and contract identifier. It consumes typed terms, events and ledger postings. The adapter supplies Wallex symbols, timestamps, precision, calendars, currency units, fee conventions and effective dates. Resolve venue support, account permission, market state and available evidence separately per action. Allow a close-only market to reduce exposure. An unknown capability remains visible with its reason.

## Delivery and acceptance

### Common decision and accounting model

Build one snapshot per decision cutoff. Its numerical view has axes of decision time, instrument and feature. Each value carries units, availability time and a validity mask. Keep raw book events for matching, a directed graph of executable currency conversions for routing, and an account journal for cash, debt and positions. Sharing BTC as an underlying links two pairs for concentration limits; their order books and liquidity remain distinct. Give instruments stable identities through symbol changes and delistings.

Only events received by the cutoff enter the snapshot. Preserve evidenced sequence order within each source. Where clocks cannot resolve a cancel/fill race, replay the plausible outcomes and report their monetary difference. Freeze the entire cross-section before scoring. Resolve ties by an explicit stable rule. Removing a competitor can change targets; reversing the storage order of the same competitors must preserve them.

The archive command [whole_market_panel.py](../gym-core/scripts/whole_market_panel.py) writes a versioned JSONL header followed by one hashed feature slice per cutoff. Omit `--symbols` for the point-in-time catalogue. Use `--symbols` for a fixed list resolved at the first cutoff. Each row includes the known product flags, catalogue revision, bid and ask, displayed quantities, quoted spread, native-currency midpoint momentum and the receipt clocks used. Missing features are null. The exploratory ranking subtracts quoted spread from momentum and requires an available ask size. It does not drive portfolio orders.

The archived week exported 168 hourly slices and 78,792 rows across 469 catalogue entries. Some market receipt was available for 373 symbols. A rank was possible at 88 cutoffs. The longest gap lasted 35 hourly cutoffs beginning November 20 at 03:30 Tehran time. The verification checked every slice digest and every feature receipt timestamp. These slices support fixed-input development comparisons. The ranking and exposure policies still need shared-account evaluation with fees and paired after-cost benchmarks.

[whole_market_rank_study.py](../gym-core/scripts/whole_market_rank_study.py) consumes those frozen slices and compares the top three symbols per quote currency with a seeded sample of the same size and a cash baseline. Each independent clip uses the existing market-order model with 500 ms submission latency, a 60-second wait, a 25% displayed-size cap and a 0.1% fee per side. Entry nonfills keep their cash. Unclosed inventory other than lot dust leaves the pair unscored. The report preserves modeled fills, fees, cash postings and residual quantities.

The archived comparison had 87 starts with eligible ranks out of 167 paired cutoffs. Only two blocks had fully scored outcomes for both policies, and neither had a fill. In the rank arm, 196 of 522 selected clips carried unscored residual inventory. The largest categories were missing an eligible exit decision (87) and insufficient exit capacity (80). This leaves the comparison inconclusive. It remains a fixed-input development comparison, with shared-account inventory feedback, exposure timing and an untouched qualification window still open.

Score three possible actions at each rebalance: retain the current portfolio, move toward cash, or trade to a new target. Evaluate incremental proceeds or costs at the requested size, including liquidation of displaced holdings. Reserve cash once across executors. Track displayed liquidity already consumed by sibling orders and document the assumptions under which a new snapshot replenishes it. Account for residual currency when a multi-leg route fails halfway. Reconcile fills, fees, financing and external flows per currency; report marked wealth separately from feasible liquidation proceeds. Retain unpriced inventory and disclose the unvalued exposure when no defensible exit bound exists.

### Experiments and decision criteria

| Research question | First comparison | Reason to stop or simplify |
|---|---|---|
| Does ranking identify executable opportunities? | Compare a fixed momentum rank, regularized linear forecast and seeded score permutations within comparable asset/liquidity groups. Fix sizing and exposure. Predict a declared horizon and charge modeled execution costs once in the account. | A favorable rank correlation without paired after-cost portfolio improvement does not justify adopting the ranker. |
| Does exposure timing add value? | Freeze the ranker. Compare a constant policy with volatility targeting and a breadth rule. Set the constant comparator to the candidate's average training exposure and carry that value into validation. | If reducing exposure by a constant amount achieves the same economic result and downside, prefer the simpler policy. |
| Can allocation or routing improve implementation? | Freeze scores and exposure. Compare equal allocation, a rebalance band and a turnover-penalized allocator. Recheck cash, rounded quantities, collateral and depth after optimization. | Retire an improvement that disappears under the predeclared adverse fee, latency or liquidity scenario at intended size. |

Run each execution comparison twice. First record a frozen stream of order intentions with size, deadline, source decision and cancellation policy. Replay it under alternative fill assumptions, recording any intention that has become infeasible. Then run the policy continuously under each assumption so inventory feedback can change later decisions. The difference between those experiments identifies where feedback matters. Vary starting capital and plot deployed cash, net return, unfilled quantity and exit time against capital. A positive result at one tiny size supports only that size range.

Fit transformations, labels with fully elapsed horizons, model coefficients and cost estimates on earlier data. Keep all markets from a timestamp in the same fold and purge training labels overlapping validation. Select a bounded set of candidates on rolling validation folds, then freeze the complete policy and retraining schedule for a later untouched test. Declare the minimum economically useful improvement, uncertainty method using time blocks, risk limits, search budget and retirement rule before evaluating that test. A short or incomplete sample yields an inconclusive result. The audited November week is development data.

One project manifest records the question, universe revision, data vintage, feature definition, baseline, parameter candidates, seed, folds and cost scenarios. Reuse cached snapshots across projects. Key fitted artifacts by training fold. The decision debugger joins frozen inputs, rejected alternatives, forecast, target, intent, fill, postings and remaining inventory. Archive all variants, including failures, under their original research question.

### Acceptance protocol

The acceptance protocol below defines the detailed checks for all three implementation milestones.

#### Run manifest and event lifecycle

| Freeze before execution | Required contents |
|---|---|
| Identity and inputs | Project, question, parent experiment, code and schema versions, source segment IDs/digests, catalogue revision, effective membership policy and data cutoff. Pin the calendar, unit conversions, feature definitions and available fee/rule vintages. |
| Account and policy | Opening free and reserved balances, liabilities and collateral by account and asset, reporting currency, starting orders/positions, lot rounding, candidate policies, benchmark policies, cost scenarios, decision cadence and data freshness limits. |
| Evaluation | Warm-up, train, validation and test intervals with explicit inclusive start and exclusive end, label horizon, seed, search budget, minimum useful effect, risk limits, valuation method and missing-data treatment. |
| Outputs | Snapshot references, rejected candidates and reason codes, target deltas, intents, fills and nonfills, postings, per-currency reconciliation, coverage report, evaluation verdict and performance measurements. Preserve attempts with errors. |

At each cutoff, ingest available events and advance venue lifecycle events under the declared ordering model. Apply fills and financing already due, snapshot positions and buying power, resolve eligible actions, then score all candidates. Reserve resources for the chosen batch. Submit intentions after the modeled decision and transport delays. Each fill references the market observation, rule vintage, fee basis, intent and ledger transaction. A metadata correction arriving tomorrow creates a new input revision. Reproduction of yesterday's run uses yesterday's revision.

#### Hand-checkable acceptance tape

Use an invented spot pair A/Q and exact decimals. Start with 100 Q and zero A. At a decision cutoff the best ask is 10 Q with 3 A available. Two buy intents request 2 A each. Declare priority in the fixture. A single snapshot supplies at most 3 A across both orders. With a 1% fee in the received asset, filling 2 A then 1 A leaves **70 Q and 2.97 A**. Explicitly cancel the remaining 1 A and release its reservation. A subsequent executable bid of 9 Q with sufficient size and no restrictive lot minimum yields **96.4627 Q** after selling 2.97 A and charging 1% of the proceeds. Equity at the bid before that exit is **96.73 Q**. These three states test capacity, fee currency and the distinction between marking and settlement.
